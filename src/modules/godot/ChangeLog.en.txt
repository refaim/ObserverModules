Version 1.0.1
-------------
 * Updated the cryptographic dependency to Mbed TLS 4.1.1 LTS and its public PSA API.
 * Refreshed the QOA source pin to Godot 4.7.2-stable; the decoder bytes remain unchanged.

Version 1.0.0
-------------
 + Added the Godot module for standalone PCK archives, embedded PCKs and same-stem EXE sidecars.
 + Encrypted directories and resources are decrypted transparently. Keys are recovered through static EXE inspection and validated with MD5; a 64-digit hexadecimal key can also be entered in Observer's password dialog.
 + Import/remap metadata restores original resource folders. [Godot] DecodeResources=1 shows converted resources instead of their compiled originals; 0 or a missing setting keeps originals. The shipped INI enables 1. Files .import and .remap remain visible in both modes.
 + Added StreamTexture, CompressedTexture2D, scaled texture containers and Image/ImageTexture conversion. Encoded PNG/WebP is identified by content and preserved; supported raw and block-compressed textures, including DXT5_RA_AS_RG, are written as PNG.
 + Added Sample, AudioStreamSample/AudioStreamWAV, MP3 and Ogg Vorbis extraction, including Godot IMA-ADPCM and QOA, to WAV, MP3 or Ogg.
 + Added FontFile extraction preserving TTF, OTF, TTC, WOFF and WOFF2 data.
 + Added RSRC and block-compressed RSCC readers. Listing inspects metadata and stops unsupported binary classes after their header; media conversion runs only during extraction. Converted entries display their packed source size and report progress against that same size.
 + Added PCK patch removal records in patch-removals.tsv and safe names with a name-map.tsv for unsafe or conflicting paths. Unrecognised resources are extracted without format conversion.
