microsoft/vcpkg

Some overlay ports under build/vcpkg/ports/ are adapted from the official
microsoft/vcpkg registry at baseline 20ceb8adbf0504d4b0d5e4058a32f9d4fc896391
(https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/20ceb8adbf0504d4b0d5e4058a32f9d4fc896391/ports/catch2):
the Catch2 recipe (portfile.cmake), its fix-install-path.patch and its vcpkg.json
manifest. The recipe here is an adaptation of that upstream recipe rather than a
verbatim copy: the Catch2 reference is moved from 3.16.0 to 3.16.1 and the
matching SHA512 digest is updated. Those files are Microsoft's build tooling and
carry the MIT grant reproduced below. This is source/build attribution for the
adapted recipe and patch, not a runtime component of the shipped modules.
